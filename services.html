Residential Sales & Purchase

Whether you're a first-time buyer or seasoned investor, our certified consultants guide you through the entire process — from shortlisting properties to completing registration. We have access to exclusive off-market listings not available anywhere else.

  • 500+ verified residential listings
  • Exclusive off-market properties

Commercial Leasing & Sales

From premium Grade A offices to high-street retail and industrial warehouses, Horizon's commercial team provides data-driven location analysis and lease negotiation services tailored to your business requirements.

  • Office spaces, co-working & retail
  • Lease negotiation & structuring
  • CAM & operational cost advisory

Property Management

Let Horizon manage your investment portfolio while you earn passive rental income. Our property management team handles tenant acquisition, maintenance, rent collection and compliance on your behalf.

  • Balance transfer advisory
