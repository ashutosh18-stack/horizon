✦ Who We Are

Built on Trust & Excellence

Horizon Properties was founded in 2009 with a single mission: to make luxury real estate accessible, transparent, and rewarding. Over 15 years, we have grown from a boutique consultancy in Gurgaon to a pan-India powerhouse with offices in 8 cities.

Our team of 200+ certified real estate professionals is dedicated to finding not just a property, but the right home or investment for every client. We believe that buying property should be an experience — not just a transaction.
